## Combined Methodology Used

Across both bug bounty programs, the following process was followed:

1. **Scope understanding:**  
   I started by reviewing the program links and identifying that each target required careful scope awareness before testing.

2. **Responsible testing mindset:**  
   I focused on safe and ethical investigation. The goal was to understand and analyse possible security issues without making unsupported claims or performing activity outside the allowed program rules.

3. **Threat and impact reasoning:**  
   I considered what kind of attacker, user impact, or business impact would be needed for a valid bug bounty report.

4. **Evidence-focused reporting:**  
   I recognised that a valid report must include reproducible steps, affected assets, clear impact, screenshots or logs where appropriate, and a practical mitigation suggestion.

5. **Avoiding overclaiming:**  
   I avoided presenting assumptions as confirmed vulnerabilities. Where the work was exploratory, it was described as attempted investigation or learning-based analysis.
